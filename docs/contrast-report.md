## Standing findings

**CF-01 — Terracotta is a fill, not a text color.**
Terracotta measures 4.88:1 on porcelain and 4.48:1 on paper. The second value is below the 4.5:1 body-text minimum. Terracotta is therefore cleared only as a button fill, a left rule, and large-text emphasis at 24px or above. Small text that needs a warm accent uses ink or moss. This is enforced in `app.css` by never assigning terracotta to a body-copy class.

**CF-02 — Porcelain and paper are close by design, so layering never relies on fill alone.**
The two surfaces differ by 1.09:1, which is intentional warmth but, as UI Plan 3.1 warns, can collapse on a low-quality panel in daylight. Every paper surface in the system therefore carries a hairline border in addition to its fill. Removing that border is a system change, not a visual preference.
